- **Reference period:** January 2025 – December 2025 (first-visit schedules). Sample: 22,594 FSUs, 2,70,472 households, 11,48,634 persons; 8,91,266 persons aged 15+ (4,96,186 rural, 3,95,080 urban). Report Table 3 and Table 4, PDF p. 37 (printed p. 7).
- **Page convention:** "PDF p." is the page index in the PDF file; "printed p." is the number printed on the page (Appendix A tables are numbered A-1, A-2, ...).
- All figures are percentages unless stated. Transgender persons (37 sample persons) are included only in "persons", not in male or female (report para 1.6.1.3).
- **Comparability:** the sample design changed from January 2025 (rotational panel, larger sample). The report says 2025 estimates "may not be strictly comparable" with earlier PLFS annual reports.
- **Activity codes** (report Appendix C, PDF p. 552–553, printed C-5 to C-6): 11 own-account worker, 12 employer, 21 unpaid helper in household enterprise, 31 regular wage/salaried, 41 casual labour in public works (usual status: all public works incl. - **Industry codes** (NIC-2008 divisions): 01-03 = agriculture, forestry, fishing (NIC section A); 05-43 = mining, manufacturing, electricity/water, construction ("secondary"); 45-96/45-99 = services ("tertiary").
- **ps vs ps+ss:** in usual status (ps+ss), a person whose principal status is not "worker" but who did economic work for 30+ days in the year as a subsidiary activity is counted as a worker. So a code-92 share under ps+ss is "domestic duties as principal status AND no subsidiary work".
